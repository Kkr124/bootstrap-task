About This Project

A clean, responsive Bootstrap remix focused on clarity & polish.

Built with Bootstrap 5 and a tiny layer of custom CSS. The goal is strong composition, consistent spacing, and accessible components.

What you’ll find inside
  • Reusable sections (hero, features, projects, contact)
  • Utilities-first spacing & type scale
  • Responsive grid with minimal overrides

Approach & Principles

Choose clarity over complexity. Compose UI from trustworthy building blocks, then polish spacing, semantics, and states.

Design Systems
Consistent tokens & sections
Performance
Lean assets, green vitals
Accessibility
Readable, keyboard-friendly
Polish
States, shadows, balance
